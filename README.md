### What I do

I lead growth and product marketing for SaaS and marketplace products. My work sits at the intersection of product, positioning, and the funnel — turning activation gaps into roadmaps, search intent into traffic, and lifecycle signals into revenue.

I own the loop end-to-end: cohort analysis, positioning, programmatic SEO, lifecycle automation, and CRO experiments. I bias toward systems that compound — content that ranks for years, lifecycle flows that pay back monthly, positioning that holds across channels.

### Growth & Product Marketing work

- **Activation & retention systems** — funnel and cohort analysis, onboarding redesign, lifecycle automation across email, in-app, and SMS.
- **Positioning & GTM** — category framing, ICP-led messaging, launch playbooks for new SKUs and AI-era search surfaces (AI Overviews / GEO).
- **Programmatic SEO** — keyword architecture, schema, internal linking, and content systems built to scale across thousands of pages.
- **CRO & experimentation** — hypothesis-driven testing on landing pages, pricing pages, and checkout flows.
- **AI-assisted marketing ops** — Claude- and ChatGPT-powered workflows for briefs, research, and content production at velocity.

### How I work

- **Find the constraint first.** Cohort and funnel analysis before tactics. Treat the bottleneck as the brief.
- **Systems over campaigns.** Programmatic SEO, lifecycle automation, and content engines compound; one-off pushes don't.
- **Positioning before paid.** If the message isn't earning attention organically, paid won't fix it.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Test, measure, decide. Move fast on signal, slow on hunch.
